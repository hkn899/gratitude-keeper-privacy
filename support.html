Getting started

Gratitude Keeper gives you a new gratitude prompt every day. Answer it, optionally add your mood and a photo, and it's saved to your journal. You can revisit past days from the History tab, and see a weekly summary from there too.

Free trial and subscription

Gratitude Keeper is free to use in full for 7 days from when you first open the app — no payment method required to start. After the trial, continuing requires one of three subscription plans (monthly, quarterly, or annual), purchased through your Apple ID via the App Store.

Managing or cancelling your subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through Apple, not by us directly. To view, change, or cancel your subscription:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions
  3. Select Gratitude Keeper and choose the option you need

You can also get there directly from inside the app: Settings → Subscription → Manage.

Restoring a purchase

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices and already have an active subscription, use the Restore Purchases button — available both on the subscription screen and under Settings in the app — to unlock it again without paying twice.

Backing up and restoring your journal

Since everything is stored only on your device, we recommend backing up periodically. Go to Settings → Backup → Export to save a file containing your full journal (including photos) wherever you like — Files, iCloud Drive, email, etc. Use Restore with that same file to bring your entries back, including onto a new device.

Daily reminders

Enable a daily notification reminding you to write from Settings → Daily reminder, and choose what time works best for you.

Your data and privacy

Your journal entries, moods, and photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including how subscription purchases are handled.
