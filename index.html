Privacy Policy for Gratitude Keeper

Effective date: August 11, 2026

Gratitude Keeper ("the app") is a private, offline gratitude journal. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Summary

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Everything you enter into Gratitude Keeper — journal entries, moods, and photos — is stored only on your device. The app has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking software of any kind.

Camera and photo library

If you choose to attach a photo to a journal entry, the app asks for permission to use your camera or photo library. The photo is copied into the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification directly on your device. This does not involve any server, and no data about you or your usage is sent anywhere to make this work.

Backup and restore

The app lets you export your journal (including any photos) to a file, and later restore from that file. This is done entirely through your device's native share sheet — you choose where the file is saved (for example, Files, iCloud Drive, or email), and we do not have access to it. Import works the same way in reverse: you pick a file from your device, and the app reads it locally.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to third parties, because the app never sends your data anywhere in the first place. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs included in the app.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the app installed. Deleting an entry removes it immediately. Uninstalling the app removes all of its locally stored data, except for any backup files you have separately exported and saved elsewhere yourself.

Children's privacy

Gratitude Keeper does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all. The app is not directed specifically at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new effective date.
